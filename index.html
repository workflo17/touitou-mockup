Where the name comes from

From the Touat oases to Tunis, Algiers, Paris and Marseille

Touitou is a Jewish surname of North African origin. The most commonly given account traces it to Touat, a group of oases in the Algerian Sahara settled by Berber Jewish communities long before the Arab conquest. Other derivations have been proposed, and the question is not settled.

From there the name spread through the Jewish communities of Algeria, Tunisia and Morocco. The largest movement came in the middle of the twentieth century, when most of North African Jewry left for France and Israel. That is why roughly seven in ten Touitous alive today are in France.

The Israeli branch is the hardest to count, because those records are kept in Hebrew and the Latin spelling splits across Touito, Tuito and Twito. Any number that shows Israel as empty is describing the limits of the dataset, not the family.
